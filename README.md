# Life, Unlocked

A cozy idle RPG about growing up: battle everyday obstacles, learn skills on the Life Grid, and unlock 66 careers.

**Play it:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/life-unlocked/

## For testers
- Works on phone and desktop browsers. No install or sign-in.
- Your progress saves in your own browser on this device. Clearing site data resets it.
- To move a save between devices: Journal → Settings → Copy save, then Import on the other device.
- Version: v0.17 (playtest build). New players get a short tutorial; tap ? at the top for tips on any screen.
- Found something? Journal → Settings → Send feedback copies your notes plus play stats.

## Feedback
Open an issue on this repository or send notes to the developer. Helpful things to mention: device and browser, what you were doing, and how long you'd been playing.
